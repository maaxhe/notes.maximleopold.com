## Anatomical location
- A4 may correspond to cytoarchitectonic area Te3 (Morosan et al., 2005). 
- supero-medial border with [[PBelt]] ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]])
- inferior neighbour [[A5]] ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]])
- medial neighbour [[TA2]] ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]])

## Function(s)
- A4 vs A5: differs in functional connectivity (this gradient defined the boundary) ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]])
- more myelin than [[A5]] (statistically), but myelin peak ≠ FC peak location ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]])
- A4 vs TA2: more myelin + different FC ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^5da523]])
	- A4 is more activated in LANGUAGE MATH and STORY contrasts ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^5da523]])
	- A4 is less activated in CUE-AVG ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^5da523]])

## Connectivity
- differs in FC to [[A5]]
- effective connectivity to [[Bachelorarbeit/2. Glasser areas/MT]] and [[MST]] ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^cef3ea]])
	- which connect to superior parietal regions forming a [[Auditory Where-Stream (Dorsal)]] involved in actions in space. ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^cef3ea]])
- functional connectivity (FC) between A4, [[PBelt]], 7AL, 7Am, 7PC ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^8fb425]])
	- auditory inputs to [[Auditory Where-Stream (Dorsal)]] ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^8fb425]])
	- maybe more shifting visual attention and eye position ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^8fb425]])
