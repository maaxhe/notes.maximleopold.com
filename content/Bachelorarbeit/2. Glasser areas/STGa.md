## Anatomical location
- on [[PPo]] and STG anterior to [[Herschl's Gyrus]] (also [[TA2]]) ([[Ahveninen et al. (2006) - PNAS#^4f3ab3]])
- part of the [[STG]]
- proposed to be part of a superior (i.e. anatomically dorsal) bank of the [[STS]] cortex 

## Function(s)
- part of the [[Auditory What-Stream (Ventral)]] ([[Ahveninen et al. (2006) - PNAS#^4f3ab3]])
- more activated in LANGUAGE STORY-MATH and TOM-RANDOM compared to [[TA2]] ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]])
- realtive to TGd is more activated in STORY and CUE-AVG ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]])

### left STGa
- showed a general main effect for the explicit compared with the implicit task, and thus might reflect a rather general evaluation of voices independent of the emotion ([[Frühholz (2015) - NeuroImage#^7e7cff]])

## Connectivity
- to [[IFG]] via strong [[Auditory What-Stream (Ventral)]] consisting of the inferior longitudinal fasciculus (ILF) and the inferior fronto-occipital fasciculus (IFOF) in its posterior portion, and the extreme capsule (EmC) in the anterior portion ([[Frühholz (2015) - NeuroImage#^0b4b98]])
- to [[IFG]] also via [[Auditory Where-Stream (Dorsal)]], but higher pathway probability via ventral compared to dorsal ([[Frühholz (2015) - NeuroImage#^b33417]])
- to left [[fOP]] by [[Auditory Where-Stream (Dorsal)]]
