## Anatomical location
- BA6, BA8, [[BA44, 44]] ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^e86488]])
- The IFJ is localized dorsal to the junction of the iPCS with the IFS. ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^e86488]])
- lesser extend BA9/46v ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^dd056c]])

![[Bedini 2023 Figure 4.png]]

## Function(s)
- procesing non-spatial information, working memory, object based ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^592b1e]]) ([[Bedini (n.d.)#^45f598]])
- sensitivity to the stimulus context and sensory domain ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^299328]])
- maintenance of goal-related information to control movement in a domain-specific way ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^299328]])
- involved in top-down attention and show evidence of sustained activity in response to a cue ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^31e24f]])
- more context dependent than [[FEF]] and influenced by bottom-up factors ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^31e24f]])
- could modulate [[DAN]] activity according to the current task demands and of toggling between [[DAN]] and [[VAN]] activities. ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^31e24f]])

## Connectivity
- the bilateral IFJ was always coactivated, 
- no FEF coactivations in the IFJ MACM results, except for the ipsilateral FEF in the LH IFJ MACM analysis. ([[Bedini (2023) - Brain Structure#^6d4ecd]])
- reached by terminations of the SLF2 and SLF3 ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^2c3952]])
- part of the [[FPN]] and language network ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^ff9022]])
- left IFJ involed 
- part of the multipledemand system, particularly the IFJp, IFJa and FEF partially ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^90b33c]])

### IFJa
- partial multiple-demand characteristics ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^31a10c]])

### IFJp
- part of the multipledemand system, particularly the IFJp, IFJa and FEF partially (area i6-8) ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^90b33c]])
- activated in relational reasoning and math task contrasts
