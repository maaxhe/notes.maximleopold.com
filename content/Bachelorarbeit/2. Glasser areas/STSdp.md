## Function(s)
- STSdp vs [[STSvp]]: 
	- more myelin + different FC ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^428d69]])
	- more activation in LANGUAGE MATH, TOM-RANDOM (esp. RH), and MOTOR CUE-AVG ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^428d69]])
- STSdp vs [[STSda]]: 
	- more myelin + distinct functional activation profile ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^428d69]])
	- more activation in CUE-AVG, RELATIONAL MATCH, working memory, SOCIAL TOM and other primary contrasts ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^428d69]])
	- less activation in STORY-MATH ([[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature#^428d69]])
- part of the semantic system #function/semantic [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^10f1f4]]
- involved in multimodal auditory and corresponding visual motion information [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^10f1f4]]

## Connectivity
