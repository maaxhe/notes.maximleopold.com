## Anatomical location
- one could think that PGi could be part of the auditory where stream due to its location in the parietal lobe posterior to A4 and STV, but [[Rolls (2022) - NeuroImage]], [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]] clearly shows more connectivity with semantic areas usually assigned to the [[Auditory What-Stream (Ventral)]].
