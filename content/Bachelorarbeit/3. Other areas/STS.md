## Function(s)
- STS is part of the semantic system #function/semantic including [[STSda]] and [[STSdp]], which is involved in multimodal auditory and corresponding visual motion information ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^10f1f4]])
	- dorsal bank STS regions are important in linking motion-related changes in the sight of the fave to the dynamically changing auditory input

## Connectivity
- dorsal bank STS is part of the [[Auditory What-Stream (Ventral)]] stream due to the semantic functions ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^821501]])
	- this processing provides evidence about *what* the message is ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^821501]]) 
- superior/dorsal bank STS enables multimodal representations including visual, auditory, and probably also somatosensory via [[PGi]], to gain access to the language system (Rolls et al. 2022e). ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^abddd4]])

### PFC targets
- connectivity to orbitofrontal/vm[[PFC]] ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^bb2bab]])
	- respond to vocalization and face movements ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^bb2bab]])
