## basal ganglia

Basal ganglia are (mostly) located between the [[insular cortex (insula)]] and the [[Thalamus]]. The internal capsule crosses between the different BG structures.

### Aufgaben

Die basal ganglia ist **zuständig für Gewohnheiten**/Habits. Sie ist dafür zuständig, dass wir routinierte Aufgaben ohne nachzudenken richtig machen. 

Auch Gebete und Fluche werden durch die basal ganglia ausgeführt. 

Das Gehirn lagert gewohnte Tätigkeiten zu den Basal Ganglien aus, um Raum im [[PFC]] freizuräumen. Warum? 
Um auf unerwartete Dinge schneller reagieren zu können. Wenn wir in einer Tätigkeit voll und ganz drin sind (also unser PFC sehr aktiv ist), haben wir kaum Raum, auf Veränderungen zu reagieren.

### other functions 
Based on the given context, the key points about the basal ganglia and its functions are as follows:

1. The basal ganglia is responsible for habits and routine tasks, allowing us to perform them without conscious effort.
2. It plays a role in executing prayers and curses.
3. The basal ganglia helps free up space in the prefrontal cortex to respond to unexpected events more quickly.
4. It is involved in [[Reward]] processing, [[Motivation]], and pleasure experiences.
5. The basal ganglia is part of the brain's reward system and integrates motivational and pleasure-related signals with motor functions.
6. It receives dopaminergic projections from the [[ventral tegmentum]] (VTA) as part of the mesolimbic dopamine pathway.
7. The [[nucleus accumbens]], a component of the basal ganglia, is often referred to as the brain's pleasure center.
8. [[Oxytocin]] receptors in the nucleus accumbens suggest its involvement in social behaviors and pair bonding.
9. The basal ganglia is involved in movement and action planning.
10. It is implicated in various diseases, such as hemiballism, which can cause chaotic and involuntary movements.
11. The [[Cerebellar Cortex]] contains different types of neurons, including stellate neurons, basket neurons, purkinje neurons, golgi neurons, and granule cells.
12. Granule cells are the only excitatory neurons in the cerebellar cortex.
13. The cerebellar cortex is organized into highly ordered repeating units called folia, which are organized into three layers: the molecular layer, the purkinje cell layer, and the granule layer.
