## Function(s)
- involved in acoustic analysis of speech ([[Friederici (2011) - Physiological Reviews#^d103c0]]) -> also eher [[Auditory What-Stream (Ventral)]]
- PT functions as a computational hub that takes input from the primary auditory cortex and performs a segregation and spectrotemporal patternmatching operation ([[Hickok & Poeppel 2007 - Nature#^ed0a3e]])

## Connectivity
- the [[Auditory Where-Stream (Dorsal)]] involves only a portion of the PT ([[Hickok & Poeppel 2007 - Nature#^98659b]])
- PT is probably not homogeneous ([[Hickok & Poeppel 2007 - Nature#^9d3308]])
