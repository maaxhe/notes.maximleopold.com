## Function(s)
- can respond to auditory stimuli such as vocalisation both in the [[STS]] regions (Baylis et al. 1987) and in the orbitofrontal cortex ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^d74388]])

## Connectivity
- orbitofrontal cortex receives connections from [[STS]] ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^d74388]])
- reward and punishment from vmPFC reach [[STS]] auditory-visual regions where objects, faces, and their semantic meaning are represented (Rolls et al. 2022e) ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^bb2bab]])
