## Auditory What-Stream (Ventral)

### Location
- starts in anterolateral [[Herschl's Gyrus]] -> [[STGa]] -> [[pPPo]] ([[Ahveninen et al. (2006) - PNAS#^0e5b5f]])
- originate in multiple [[STG]] seed regions and prob. terminate in anterior/posterior [[IFG]] (Fruhholz and Grandjean, 2013b) ([[Frühholz (2015) - NeuroImage#^bd4989]])
- [[STS]] -> pITL, portions of the MTG and ITG

### Function
- involved in decoding of spectrally complex sounds ("auditory objects) which includes the decoding of speech sounds ("speech perception") and their ultimate linking to meaning in humans. ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^e44819]])
- the ventral pathway contains hierarchical circuits that are specialized for auditory perception and scene analysis.
	- -> proposed model: heirarchie in the ventral stream. first encoding then perceptual judgements  ([[Cohen et al. (2016) - Springer#^de2ca8]])
- sound-invariant meaning, e.g. speech semantics ([[Frühholz (2015) - NeuroImage#^fadf2d]], [[Rauschecker & Scott (2009) - Nature Neuroscience]])
