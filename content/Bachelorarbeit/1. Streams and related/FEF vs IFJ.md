## FEF vs IFJ
eine extra Seite, weil das wichtig ist: 

- both localized within the same gradient of the rostro-caudal axis. ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^299328]])
- FEF and IFJ dynamically interact with subcortical and thalamic structures to achieve efficient control of behaviour (Halassa & Kastner, 2017; White et al., 2017). ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^299328]])
- both are involved in top-down attention and show evidence of sustained activity in response to a cue ([[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)#^31e24f]])
