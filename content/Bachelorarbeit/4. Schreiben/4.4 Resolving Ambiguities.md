# 4.4 Resolving Ambiguities
During the analysis, areas A4 or PSL seem to have an ambiguous connectivity pattern. This chapter will dissolve ambiguities found in the literature and results of the RSFC analysis. 
## 4.4.1 A4 and A5 
We placed areas A4 and A5 in the 'where'-stream (Section 3.2.5), given the conflicting evidence in the literature ([[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]], [[Glasser et al. (2016) - Nature]]). Partial correrlation anaylsis resolving this conflict empirically by showing robust and bilateral connectivity of both areas A4 and A5 with IFJa (A4: left $z = 8.25$, right $z = 8.76$; A5: left $z = 11.63$, right $z = 6.96$), while showing no meaningful coupling with FEF. The only exception is a weak, left-lateralized A4-FEF connection in the single seed analysis with FEF. This pattern positions both A5 and A5 as 'what'-stream gateways under potential IFJa top-down control. 
## 4.4.2 PSL 
[[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]] classify PSL within the ventral semantic network, while [[Dureux (2024)]] reports complete acoustic unresponsiveness across all stimulus categories. Our analysis reveals a strongly asymmetric connectivity profile. The IFJa single-seed analysis shows bilateral PSL-IFJa coupling (left $z = 9.65$, right $z = 13.06$, $mean = 0.01$). The FEF single-seed analysis reveals a right-lateralized PSL-FEF connection ($z = 21.37$, $mean = 0.03$), entirely absent in the left hemisphere. No other ROI in this analysis displays this pattern of bilateral 'what'-stream coupling alongside exclusive right-lateralized 'where'-stream coupling.
## 4.4.3 STV
STV, classified as part of the ventral language network by [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]], couples directly with both prefrontal seeds in separate single-seed analyses. In the IFJa analysis, STV couples bilaterally with IFJa (left $z = 18.90$, $\text{mean} = 0.02$; right $z = 18.55$, $\text{mean} = 0.02$), after partialling out FEF. In the FEF analysis, STV couples bilaterally with FEF (left $z = 19.50$, $\text{mean} = 0.02$; right $z = 17.82$, $\text{mean} = 0.02$), after partialling out IFJa. In both cases, the coupling is direct, not mediated through indirect connections.
