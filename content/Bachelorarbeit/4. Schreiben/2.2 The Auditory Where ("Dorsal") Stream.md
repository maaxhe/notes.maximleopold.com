# 2.2 The Auditory Where ("Dorsal") Stream

## 2.2.1. The Debate How vs Where
The function of the where-stream is debated ([[Hickok & Poeppel (2004) - Cognition#^9986e5]]), but due to its connection and location to the motor cortey, it is critical for audio-motor integration, similar to visual domain ([[Hickok & Poeppel (2004) - Cognition#^97c4be]]). This includes mapping sound onto articulatory-based representations. ([[Hickok & Poeppel (2004) - Cognition#^c275cd]]). The suggestion that the dorsal stream has an auditory–motor integration function differs from earlier arguments for a dorsal auditory ‘where’ system ([[Hickok & Poeppel 2007 - Nature#^0af4d2]]).

## 2.2.2 Anatomical Definition and Evidence
- **Frontal Eye Field ([[FEF]]):** Located in the caudal middle frontal gyrus, ventral to the junction of the superior precentral sulcus (sPCS) and superior frontal sulcus (SFS). The FEF contains topographic maps of contralateral space and is a core node of the Dorsal Attention Network ([[DAN]]), responsible for spatial attentional control.

## 2.2.3 Broca's area connecting to the where-stream
**Area 44** shows connectivity to A4, PBelt and A5 and might form a language-related where (dorsal) stream, according to [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex#^cef3ea]]. 


## 2.2.4 dorsal language stream vs auditory where stream 
Rolls and Friederici talk about a dorsal language-stream, terminating in BA44. The question arises whether the dorsal language pathway also belongs to the auditory where stream, since the language pathway focusses mainly on language, which is a ventral domain. and where stream is rather spatial. nonetheless, there does not need to be such a clear distiction and those streams could be intertwined. this is why we will treat the language dorsal stream first as a aufitory where stream. Hickock and Poeppel argue that the dorsal stream is primarily for audio-motoer integration and this also relates to speech production there could be evidence for the dorsal language-stream being part of an overall auditory where-stream. this will be tested in chapter [...] with BA44 and BA45 as seed regions observing their functional conenctivity.
