# 4.1 Global Connectivity Patterns
Before disentangling the auditory streams, we validated whether our selected prefrontal seed regions (FEF and IFJa) functionally correspond to the established spuramodal attention networks described in the literature. 
## 4.1.1 Validation of Prefrontal Seed Regions
To validate the anatomical specificity of our seed regions, we compared their intrinsic connectivity fingerprints against established networks (Figure [[FEF vs IFJa full connectivity.png]])

- **FEF:** The FEF seed exhibits robust full correlation with the superior parietal lobe ([[7AL]], [[7Am]], [[7PC]]). This replicates the where-stream, similar to the visual stream [[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)]], together with auditory and motor regions such as A5, PBelt, FOP1, STV and PSL
- **IFJa:** In contrast, the IFJa showed strong coupling with the anterior language network (HCP language task)

In the analysis we compared the two seed regions FEF and IFJa in both hemispheres. Looking at functional connectivity with full correlation we found high correlation of FEF to spatial auditory areas according to [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]] where [[7AL]], [[7Am]], [[7PC]] might play a role in spatial orientation within the auditory where-stream. This connectivity might be running via the [[SLF]]2 (Superior Longitudinal Fasculus), partly SLF3 ([[Bedini (n.d.)#^def992]]), which mediates spatial-motor and auditory communication. 

First if we look at functional connectivity we see a clear pattern that connects the [[FEF]] to the [[Where-pathway]] and auditory areas.

## 4.1.2 Results of the Broca-Seed Validation
The partial correaltion analysis of area [[BA44, 44]] and [[BA45, 45]] revealed two distinct connectivity fingerprints that align with models from [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]]:

- **Area 44 (Dorsal hub):** the connectivity profile shows significant coupling to the inferior parietal cluster (PF, PFop, PFcm) and motion related regions ([[MT]]/[[MST]]). Prefrontal coupling is expressed through robust connectivity to [[IFJ]]a $z= 11.15$ in the left hemisphere ($z= 8.93$ in the right), but remained statistically disconnected from the [[FEF]]. 
	- This confirms area 44's role in the where-stream rather than pure semantic/language related coupling. 
- **Area 45 (Ventral):** The connectivity profile shows a massive extension into the temporal lobe with ligh z-scores for STGa, STSda, STSdp and TPOJ1. Also strong connections to the Persisylvian Language Area ([[PSL]] $z \approx 5.36$ to $16.91$) and [[BA47l, 47l]] ($z = 24.45$ in the left hemisphere).
	- This anchors area 45 in the definitive group if the auditory what-stream regions. 

![[44 vs 45 part corr.png]]

### 4.1.2.1 Absence of FEF-44 connectivity 
A significant observation in our validation is the lack of direct partial correlation between the FEF and area 44. While Rolls might label both as dorsal, our analysis suggests a more nuanced functional hierarchy. 

- **Spatial vs Structural Control:** The FEF appreas strictly limited ti the spatial "where" network (7PC, 7Am) while the IFJa manages the entire informational and structural complex of the Broca-area. 
- The fact that 44 shows no connectivity to spatial regions as the FEF does, this suggests that the dorsal language stream is functionally distinct from the spatial attention network directed by FEF.
