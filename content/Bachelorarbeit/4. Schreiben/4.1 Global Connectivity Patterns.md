# 4.1 Global Connectivity Patterns
Before disentangling the auditory streams, we validated whether our selected prefrontal seed regions (FEF and IFJa) functionally correspond to the established spuramodal attention networks described in the literature. 
## 4.1.1 Validation of Prefrontal Seed Regions
To validate the anatomical specificity of our seed regions, we compared their intrinsic connectivity fingerprints against established networks (Figure [[FEF vs IFJa full connectivity.png]])

- **FEF:** The FEF seed exhibits robust full correlation with the superior parietal lobe ([[7AL]], [[7Am]], [[7PC]]). This replicates the where-stream, similar to the visual stream [[Bedini & Baldauf (2021)]], together with auditory and motor regions such as A5, PBelt, FOP1, STV and PSL
- **IFJa:** In contrast, the IFJa showed strong coupling with the anterior language network (HCP language task)

In the analysis we compared the two seed regions FEF and IFJa in both hemispheres. Looking at functional connectivity with full correlation we found high correlation of FEF to spatial auditory areas according to [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]] where [[7AL]], [[7Am]], [[7PC]] might play a role in spatial orientation within the auditory where-stream. This connectivity might be running via the [[SLF]]2 (Superior Longitudinal Fasculus), partly SLF3 ([[Bedini (n.d.)#^def992]]), which mediates spatial-motor and auditory communication. 

First if we look at functional connectivity we see a clear pattern that connects the [[FEF]] to the [[Where-pathway]] and auditory areas.
