# 4.1 Global Connectivity Patterns

## 4.1.1 FEF vs. IFJa connectivity
In the analysis we compared the two seed regions FEF and IFJa in both hemispheres. Looking at functional connectivity with full correlation we found high correlation of FEF to spatial auditory areas according to [[Rolls et al. (2023) - Cerebral Cortex]] where [[7AL]], [[7Am]], [[7PC]] might play a role in spatial orientation within the auditory where-stream. This connectivity might be running via the [[SLF]]2 (Superior Longitudinal Fasculus), partly SLF3 ([[Bedini (n.d.)#^def992]]), which mediates spatial-motor and auditory communication.
