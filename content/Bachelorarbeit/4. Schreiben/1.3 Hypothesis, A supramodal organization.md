# 1.3 Hypothesis, A supramodal organization

We hypothesize that the functional connections of the auditory [what](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/1.-Streams-and-related/Auditory-What-Stream-\(Ventral\)) and [where](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/1.-Streams-and-related/Auditory-Where-Stream-\(Dorsal\)) streams are supramodal within the prefrontal cortex.

In detail:

- The seeds of the [Frontal Eye Field (FEF)](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/2.-Glasser-areas/FEF) should preferentially connect to the auditory [where-stream](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/1.-Streams-and-related/Auditory-Where-Stream-\(Dorsal\))
- The seeds of the [Inferior Frontal Junction (IFJ)](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/2.-Glasser-areas/IFJ) should preferentially connect to the auditory [what-stream](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/1.-Streams-and-related/Auditory-What-Stream-\(Ventral\))

We expect this organization to be frequency-specific, with alpha and beta rhythms coordinating the two streams. Showing that not there is a structural and also a rhythmically gated functional segregation.

Because both [FEF](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/2.-Glasser-areas/FEF) and [IFJ](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/2.-Glasser-areas/IFJ) are known to direct top-down attentional control in the visual domain, we propose that they may play an analogous role for auditory attention as well along the [where](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/1.-Streams-and-related/Auditory-Where-Stream-\(Dorsal\)) and [what](https://notes.maximleopold.com/Bachelorarbeit/1.-Streams-and-related/Auditory-What-Stream-\(Ventral\)) pathways.

In summary we test whether the prefrontal cortex shows the same division of attention direction in the auditory stream as is found in the visual stream.
