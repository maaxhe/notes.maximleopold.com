# 4.2 Testing the "Where" Stream (FEF Connectivity)
To evaluate the precise anatomical pathways of the top-down auditory attention network, the functional connectivity was  full and partial correlation shows a clear spatial-dorsal pattern, placing FEF in its known framework.

## 4.2.1 Inferior Parietal Connection
In visual stream models, FEF couples strongly with the superior parietal lobule areas 7PC, 7AM and 7AL. However, partial correlation reveals a stark contrast, not showing significant coupling of FEF to superior parietal regions 7AL, 7AM and 7PC, although still coupling bilaterally with inferior parietal regions of PF (left $z = 13.89$, $\text{mean} = 0.02$; right $z = 18.22$, $\text{mean} = 0.03$), PFop and PFcm. 
## 4.2.2 Premotor cluster ("How" Stream)
The strongest connections with FEF are the premotor and frontal opercular areas. The SCEF (left $z = 24.19$, $\text{mean} = 0.04$; right $z = 24.55$, $\text{mean} = 0.06$) and area 55b (left $z = 24.52$, $\text{mean} = 0.08$; right $z = 24.55$, $\text{mean} = 0.09$) form a bilateral motor-premotor cluster, along with FOP1 and area 43. Together these areas might form the "how" stream, proposed by [[Hickok & Poeppel 2007 - Nature]], connecting spatial information with motor action plans. 

## 4.2.3 Resolving Superior Parietal Connections 
A crucial topological missconception emerges, when comparing full and partial correaltion profiles. In the ful correaltion, the FEF exhibits strong coupling with superior parietal areas (e.g., 7Am: left $z = 24.52$; 7PC: left $z = 24.47$). However, the partial correlation model, these conenctions vanish entirely. This demonstrates that FEF does not maintain full spatial connections with superior parietal lobule when partialling out auditory ROIs. For auditory-spatial information, FEF mainly recruits Inferior Parietal Lobule (PF/PFop) and motor-sensitive areas.
