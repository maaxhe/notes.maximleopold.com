A significant observation in our validation is the lack of direct partial correlation between the FEF and area 44. While Rolls might label both as dorsal, our analysis suggests a more nuanced functional hierarchy. 

- **Spatial vs Structural Control:** The FEF appreas strictly limited ti the spatial "where" network (7PC, 7Am) while the IFJa manages the entire informational and structural complex of the Broca-area. 
- The fact that 44 shows no connectivity to spatial regions as the FEF does, this suggests that the dorsal language stream is functionally distinct from the spatial attention network directed by FEF.
