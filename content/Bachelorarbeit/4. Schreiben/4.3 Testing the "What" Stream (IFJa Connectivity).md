# 4.3 Testing the "What" Stream (IFJa Connectivity)
In the human brain, language takes a large chuck of cognitive workload, which we can see due to the over-balance towards the ventral what-stream. 
In the ventral what-stream we se a strong connectivity within the semantic system. Especially this what-stream is langauge-heavy due to its conenction to the frontal Broca's areas 44, 45 and 47l.

### STV surprise
Rolls claims [[STV]] is part of the language-realted semantic pathway connecting to [[BA45, 45]]


here I contrasted STV to [[PSL]] so see where STV is connected differently than the where-stream PSL region. 

In functional connectivity with full correlation it is interesting to see that in left hemisphere STV shows a strong connection to the where-stream whereas in right hemisphere it is strongly oriented towards the what-stream.
