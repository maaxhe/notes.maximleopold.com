## 7.2 Final ROI Classification

The following table lists all 36 target regions of interest included in the analyses, with their final stream assignments as determined by partial correlation analysis. Three areas — A4, PSL, and STV — showed connectivity profiles that differed from their initial pre-analysis classification (Section 3.2) and are marked with †. The rationale for each reclassification is discussed in Sections 4.4 and 5.4.

### Table A1: Prefrontal Seed Regions

| **Area** | **Full Name** | **Role** |
| -------- | ------------- | -------- |
| FEF | Frontal Eye Field | Primary seed — where-stream controller |
| IFJa | Anterior Inferior Frontal Junction | Primary seed — what-stream controller |
| IFJp | Posterior Inferior Frontal Junction | Control seed |
