## Van Essen et al. (2013)

**Core argument:** This is the primary overview paper for the WU-Minn Human Connectome Project. It describes the scientific goals, acquisition protocols, preprocessing pipelines, and data-sharing infrastructure of the HCP. It is the standard citation for any study using HCP data.

**Key details relevant to thesis:**
- Introduces the HCP 3T Connectome Skyra scanner and the customized acquisition sequences (including the rs-fMRI parameters: TR=720ms, TE=33.1ms, 2mm isotropic, multi-band factor=8)
- Describes the minimal preprocessing pipeline (spatial distortion correction, motion correction, B0 field correction, surface registration via MSMAll)
- Introduces the fs_LR 32k grayordinate space used for CIFTI dense time series
- Justifies the large-sample design (N=1200 subjects, twin/sibling family structure) for heritability and individual differences analyses
- Companion paper to Glasser et al. (2016), Barch et al. (2013), and Smith et al. (2013) — together these four form the core HCP citation cluster

**How to cite:**
- §3.1.1: cite alongside Wu-Minn HCP Consortium (2018) when introducing the HCP S1200 dataset — Van Essen (2013) for the project overview/design, Wu-Minn (2018) for the specific S1200 release manual
- Soyuhos (2026) cites Van Essen (2013) as the primary HCP reference; all HCP-based papers standardly cite this paper
